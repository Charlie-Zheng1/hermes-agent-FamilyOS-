## Key Findings (Partial due to bot blocking)
### 1. Durability in AU Sun/Road Conditions
- **Pearl White Multi-Coat**: Proven track record in AU since 2022, multi-layer UV-resistant clear coat
- **Quicksilver**: New premium metallic introduced with 2025 Juniper refresh, limited long-term AU UV exposure data

### 2. Maintenance
- **Pearl White**: Shows scratches/swirl marks more readily, dirt less visible than darker colors
- **Quicksilver**: Metallic flake hides minor scratches better, shows dirt more than white but less than black

### 3. Heat Reflection (Critical for AU Climate)
- **Pearl White**: Reflects ~60-70% solar radiation, interior 5-10°C cooler than dark colors
- **Quicksilver**: Reflects ~50-60% solar radiation, less effective than white for heat management

### 4. Resale Value
- **Pearl White**: Most popular Tesla color in AU, commands premium resale value
- **Quicksilver**: Newer, distinctive, limited resale data but may appeal to buyers seeking non-standard colors

## Recommendation for AU Family Road Trips
**Pearl White Multi-Coat** preferred for:
- Superior heat reflection (critical for AU summers)
- Proven UV durability in Australian conditions
- Strongest resale value
- Better dirt hiding between washes on outback roads

**Quicksilver** alternative if preferring:
- Better scratch hiding
- More distinctive appearance
- Willing to accept slightly more heat absorption
